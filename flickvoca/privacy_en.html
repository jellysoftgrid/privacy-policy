FlickVoca Privacy Policy

1. Overview

JellySoftGrid ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") values your privacy. We are committed to protecting personal information in compliance with applicable data protection regulations and the Google Play Developer Policies. This Privacy Policy transparently explains how FlickVoca ("App") collects, uses, stores, and disposes of user information.

By default, the App is designed as an offline-accessible vocabulary learning tool where all core flashcards and forgetting curve intervals are fully usable as a guest without creating an account. We collect and process minimal personal information only when users voluntarily activate optional features such as Google Sign-In and Cloud Backup, or via integrated analytics SDKs.

2. Information Collected and Purpose of Collection

A. Google Sign-In & Cloud Backup (Optional)

  • Information Collected: Google unique user identifier (User ID), email address, profile display name, and vocabulary learning progress data (number of studied words, spaced-repetition review logs, interval weights, onboarding level, session settings).
  • Purpose: Securely syncing and restoring user learning progression across devices or following reinstallation.
  • Collection Method: Collected via the Google Sign-In SDK and Firebase Authentication / Cloud Firestore when the user initiates [Continue with Google] or [Settings > Account > Connect Google Account].

B. Advertising Services (Google AdMob)

  • Information Collected: Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / IDFA), coarse geographic location derived from IP address, device specifications (manufacturer, model, OS version), and ad interaction/view metrics.
  • Purpose: Serving interstitial and banner ads to sustain free educational services, managing ad frequency capping, measuring advertising performance, and preventing fraudulent/invalid traffic.
  • Collection Method: Automatically collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

C. Analytics and Service Improvement (Amplitude Analytics)

  • Information Collected: Pseudonymized device instance ID, app launch and session timestamps, session completion summary telemetry (cards reviewed, known/ambiguous/unknown counts, unassisted recall count, average response time in ms, session duration), screen view logs, and app version.
  • Purpose: Calibrating word difficulty bands, optimizing the response-weighted Ebbinghaus forgetting curve algorithm, identifying technical anomalies, and improving overall user experience.
  • Collection Method: Automatically collected via the Amplitude Analytics SDK. Telemetry is aggregated at the session level rather than tracking individual cards, protecting user privacy.

D. Device Local Storage & Caching (Local Storage & IndexedDB)

  • Information Stored: Local flashcard study queue and review progress, native TTS pronunciation audio cache, word illustration cache, and UI preferences (blind mode, auto-speak, swipe direction).
  • Purpose: Providing zero-latency 26-second rapid skimming even in offline environments and minimizing unnecessary network bandwidth usage.
  • Storage Method: Stored locally on the user's device via browser Local Storage and Service Worker Cache API.

3. Retention and Disposal Period

The Developer retains personal information only for the duration necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. If a user executes [Settings > Account > Delete Account] or requests account removal, all associated cloud records in Firestore are permanently purged without undue delay.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing & International Transfers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. To provide essential cloud synchronization and quality analytics, data is processed by the following verified global third-party service providers:

Recipient Purpose Country of Processing Privacy Policy
Google LLC Firebase Cloud Firestore (cloud data sync), Firebase Auth (authentication), Google AdMob (ad delivery and measurement) United States Google Privacy Policy
Amplitude, Inc. Learning session telemetry and aggregated analytics United States Amplitude Privacy Policy

5. Data Destruction Procedures and Methods

Electronically stored data is permanently deleted using irreversible technical methods. Users can immediately delete their cloud-stored study data at any time by selecting the [Delete Account] button in the in-app Settings menu. Locally cached data can also be wiped at any time by clearing application data or browser cache.

6. User Rights and Choices

Users have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their cloud backup data at any time. Users can delete their data in real time via the in-app account deletion feature, or by contacting our support email.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 14 in South Korea, or the equivalent minimum age in relevant jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that personal information of a child has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete such data.
