Moonrabbit Sudoku Privacy Policy

1. Overview

JellySoftGrid ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") values your privacy. We are committed to protecting personal information in compliance with applicable data protection regulations and the Google Play Developer Policies. This Privacy Policy transparently explains how Moonrabbit Sudoku ("App") collects, uses, stores, and disposes of user information.

By default, the App is designed as an offline-first game where all core puzzles and gameplay are fully accessible as a guest without creating an account. We collect and process minimal personal information only when users voluntarily activate optional features such as Google Sign-In and Cloud Backup, or via integrated advertising and analytics SDKs.

2. Information Collected and Purpose of Collection

A. Google Sign-In & Cloud Backup (Optional)

  • Information Collected: Google unique user identifier (User ID), email address, profile display name, and gameplay progression data (player level, EXP, collection unlock status, best clear time records).
  • Purpose: Securely syncing and restoring user game progression across devices or following reinstallation.
  • Collection Method: Collected via the Google Sign-In SDK and Firebase Authentication when the user initiates [Settings > Connect with Google Account].

B. Advertising Services (Google AdMob)

  • Information Collected: Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / IDFA), coarse geographic location derived from IP address, device specifications (manufacturer, model, OS version), and ad interaction/view metrics.
  • Purpose: Serving banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads (unlocking bonus collections), measuring ad performance, and preventing fraudulent/invalid traffic.
  • Collection Method: Automatically collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

C. Analytics and Error Reporting (Amplitude Analytics & Firebase)

  • Information Collected: Pseudonymized device instance ID, app launch and session duration, puzzle completion and failure statistics, screen view telemetry, and application crash reports.
  • Purpose: Balancing puzzle difficulty, identifying technical defects, and optimizing application performance.
  • Collection Method: Automatically collected via the Amplitude React Native SDK and Firebase SDK.

3. Retention and Disposal Period

The Developer retains personal information only for the duration necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. If a user executes [Settings > Reset All Data and Settings], disconnects their Google Account, or requests account deletion, all associated cloud records are permanently purged without undue delay, unless retention is specifically required by applicable law.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing & International Transfers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. To provide essential game services and reliable infrastructure, data is processed by the following verified global third-party service providers:

Recipient Purpose Country of Processing Privacy Policy
Google LLC Firebase Cloud Firestore, Firebase Auth, Google AdMob ad delivery United States Google Privacy Policy
Amplitude, Inc. In-game funnel analysis and gameplay telemetry United States Amplitude Privacy Policy

5. Data Destruction Procedures and Methods

Electronically stored data is permanently deleted using irreversible technical methods. Users can immediately delete all locally stored game progression, stats, and configurations at any time by selecting the [Reset All Data and Settings] button in the in-app Settings menu.

6. User Rights and Choices

Users have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their cloud backup data at any time. Users can opt out of personalized advertising or reset their advertising identifier directly in their device operating system settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID"
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > toggle off tracking permissions

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 14 in South Korea, or the equivalent minimum age in relevant jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that personal information of a child has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete such data.
